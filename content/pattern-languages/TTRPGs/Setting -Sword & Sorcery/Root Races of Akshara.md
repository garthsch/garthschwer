# The Root Races of Akshara

The **Root Races** of Akshara represent the cyclical "descent into matter," a metaphysical process where life evolved from pure energy into dense physical form, resulting in civilizations that grow increasingly material but spiritually blind.

## The Cycle of Descent

Unlike standard evolutionary models, the history of Akshara is defined by the **Akashic Descent**. Early races possessed immense spiritual power but lacked physical permanence. Later races gained durability and reason but lost their connection to the ether, leaving them vulnerable to the [[Cosmic Horror|Mythos]] entities waiting beyond the veil.

## The Second Root: The Saurians
*Also known as the Anunnaki, Naga, or the Cold Ones.*

The Second Root Race marks the transition from elemental energy to astral biology. They were not evolved, but sculpted by the dark god [[Set]] on the lost continent of **Mu**. Because they were forged rather than born, they lack the chaotic spark of true life, possessing instead a cold, crystalline intellect.

* **Nature:** Saurians are driven by "limitless ambition." They view other life forms not as equals, but as raw resources to be enslaved or reshaped. Their civilization was built on the domination of the Astral Plane.
* **The God Wars:** Their empire collapsed during the **God Wars**, where they were decimated by the planetary defense mechanisms known as the [[Orixa]].
* **Legacy:** In the modern age, they are few in number but incredibly dangerous. They operate from the shadows, wielding **Dark Magic** and seeking to reopen the gates to the Void.

## The Third Root: The Lemurians
*Also known as the Flesh-Shapers, The Uplifted, or Children of the Lawgiver.* NEEDS TO SPECIFICALLY INDICATE THAT THESE ARE THE SIMIAN APE-MEN.

Originating on the continent of **Lemuria**, this race represents the world's first true biological mastery. Their progenitor, the enigmatic **[[Lawgiver]]**, sacrificed their own immortality to "uplift" the primal simian stock of the island into sentience.

* **Biomancy:** Rejecting the stone and metal of the Dvergar and the dark astral arts of the Saurians, Lemurians became masters of **Biomancy**. They grow their cities from massive trees, shape bones into weapons, and gene-splice riding mounts from alchemical slurry.
* **The Great War:** They engaged in a millennia-long conflict with the Saurians, a war of Biology versus Sorcery.
* **Isolation:** Their Golden Age ended not with a bang, but a whimper—a virulent plague engineered by fighting Lemurian tribes that wiped out the majority of their population. The survivors retreated into deep isolation, becoming introspective scholars who guard their gene-banks and biological technologies with zeal.
