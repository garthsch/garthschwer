# Languages of the Yellow Sign: Linguistic Stratification

In the **Akshara**, language operates as a metaphysical index of spiritual descent. A race's native tongue does not merely facilitate communication; it defines their resonant frequency with the **Elemental Titans**, the corruption of the **Cthulhu Mythos**, and the density of the physical earth.

## 1. The High Speech (The Source Code)

The language of the **1st Root Race** (Elemental Titans) serves as the programming language of reality. It is not spoken conversationally but enacted ritually.

-   **Linguistic Model:** **Sumerian** (Language Isolate).
-   **Structural Logic:** **Agglutinative**. Sorcery is the act of chaining precise suffixes to root concepts to legislate local reality. A spell is not a request; it is a complex sentence structure that binds energy to matter (e.g., constructing the phrase *"Fire-that-burns-flesh-but-spares-stone"*).
-   **Metaphysical Risk:** To speak the High Tongue is to vibrate the ether. A "Magic Mishap" occurs when the intricate chain of morphemes breaks, drawing the attention of a slumbering Titan.
-   **Artifacts:** Spells are preserved as cuneiform script pressed into clay or **Orichalcum** tablets, physically grounding the volatile syntax.

## 3. The Nephilim Tongue (The Language of Carcosa)

The Nephilim language, modeled on **Etruscan**, is engineered to reflect the **Gothic Horror** and **Non-Euclidean** geometry of their dying metropolis. It is a language of ghosts and echoes.

### Key Characteristics

-   **The Breath of the Void:** The phonology avoids hard, grounded sounds. Words are whispered or hissed, requiring masks to resonate properly within the skull.
-   **Circular Time:** The grammar lacks a linear Past or Future tense. Verbs are conjugated only for **The Immediate** (Now) and **The Eternal** (Always/Never), reflecting a history that is a spiral rather than a line.
-   **Vertical Orthography:** The script is written in vertical columns that hang like stalactites, mirroring the spires of the city.
