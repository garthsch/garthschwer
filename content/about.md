## Who I am

I'm Garth Schwer — a cybersecurity leader with an MBA in Data & Cyber Management, currently working at the intersection of security operations and executive strategy.

Professionally, I've built and led SOC teams, developed cybersecurity policy, and worked to bridge the gap between technical practitioners and the executives who fund and govern their work.

## What this is

This is a digital garden — a working notebook where I think in public. It's not a blog. Posts aren't polished or final. Ideas grow here over time:

- 🌱 **Seedlings** — barely formed, possibly wrong
- 🪴 **Saplings** — taking shape but not yet tested
- 🌳 **Evergreen** — ideas I've pressure-tested enough to stand behind

The deeper thread connecting everything here is a conviction: classical thinking systems — philosophy, contemplative practice, narrative pattern recognition — are becoming the essential human skills in a world increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence.

AI is extraordinary at processing, summarizing, and generating. But the skills that make humans irreplaceable — asking the right questions, holding paradox, recognizing deep patterns across domains, making meaning — come from traditions that are centuries or millennia old.

That's what I explore here.
