This is where I think in public.

Some of these ideas are 🌱 seedlings — barely formed, possibly wrong.
Some are 🪴 saplings — taking shape but not yet tested.
A few are 🌳 evergreen — ideas I've pressure-tested enough to stand behind.

None of them are finished. That's the point.

I'm a cybersecurity leader with an MBA in Data & Cyber Management. Professionally, I bridge technical operations and executive strategy. But the deeper thread running through everything here is a conviction that classical thinking systems — philosophy, contemplative practice, narrative pattern recognition — are becoming the essential human skills in a world increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence.

**[[classical-thinking/index|Classical Thinking]]** — Philosophy, meditation, martial arts, integral theory. The thinking systems that AI can't replicate.

**[[pattern-languages/index|Pattern Languages]]** — Mythology, worldbuilding, narrative. How humans have always made sense of complexity.

**[[the-workshop/index|The Workshop]]** — How I build, think, and manage knowledge.
