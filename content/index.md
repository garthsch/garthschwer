This is where I think in public.

Some of these ideas are 🌱 seedlings — barely formed, possibly wrong. Some are 🪴 saplings — taking shape but not yet tested. A few are 🌳 evergreen — ideas I've pressure-tested enough to stand behind.

*None of them are finished. That's the point.*

I'm a cybersecurity leader with an MBA in Data & Cyber Management. Professionally, I bridge technical operations and executive strategy. But the deeper thread running through everything here is a conviction that classical thinking systems — philosophy, contemplative practice, narrative pattern recognition — are becoming the essential human skills in a world increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence.
