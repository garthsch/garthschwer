# Why Tech Workers Love Fantasy

**Context**: Why does [[Silicon Valley]] gravitate toward High Fantasy (e.g., [[Lord of the Rings]]) over [[Science Fiction]]?

## 1. Mastery of Complex Systems
Tech workers are professional **system thinkers**.
- **The Appeal**: High Fantasy is dense with rules and magic systems.
- **The Connection**: Learning lore feels like learning a new **codebase**. It is "recreational data processing."

## 2. Binary Morality
- **The Appeal**: Fantasy offers clear Good vs. Evil.
- **The Connection**: Modern tech ethics are ambiguous. Fantasy provides [[Moral Clarity]].

## 3. The Wizard Archetype
- **The Appeal**: Wizards gain power through **hidden knowledge**.
- **The Connection**: Programmers type "incantations" (code) to alter reality. [[Gandalf]] is the ultimate senior engineer.

## 4. World-Building as Engineering
- **The Appeal**: Creating a universe from scratch.
- **The Connection**: Mirrors the desire to build a [[Metaverse]] where you define the rules.

## 5. Rejection of the Present
- **The Appeal**: Pastoral and tactile.
- **The Connection**: A reaction against screen fatigue and hyper-modernity.
