Your AI moat is turning into a swamp. Here's what that looks like:

Google and OpenAI are racing to lock you in through personal context.
Context you can't see, can't edit, can't audit.

The pitch: "Our model learns you. Gets smarter over time."

Here’s what actually happened to me.

I’m a cybersecurity leader, so Gemini started dropping cyberpunk
references into everything. Cute at first. Noise after a week.

I had a sword & sorcery phase last year. Now it keeps surfacing my embarrassing fan fiction drafts as "relevant context."

That's not personalization. That’s pollution.

Three signs your AI context is working against you:

1. The model references projects you've moved on from.
2. Personalization feels like being stereotyped, not understood.
3. You're correcting the AI’s assumptions instead of using its outputs.

The context you can't audit is the context that corrupts your work.

The real moat isn’t lock-in. It's intentional control over the context.

What's your experience? Is AI personalization actually sharpening your outputs, or is it just making the model feel familiar while quality quietly drifts?
